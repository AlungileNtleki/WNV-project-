# Comparative analysis of Envelope protein amino-Acid substitutions in West Nile Virus Lineage 2 across historical and novel geographical settings

## Project Overview

West Nile virus (WNV) is a mosquito-borne flavivirus of public health and veterinary importance. This study focuses on West Nile virus lineage 2, investigating envelope (E) protein amino-acid substitutions and selection pressures across historical and novel geographical ranges.

The study compares two geographically defined groups:

- **Historical range:** Sub-Saharan Africa and Madagascar.
- **Novel range:** Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

The research examines envelope protein amino-acid diversity, identifies codon sites showing evidence of positive selection, and investigates the structural context of selected substitutions. Three-dimensional protein modelling is used to assess whether variable residues occur in functionally important regions of the envelope protein.

## Research Objectives and Aim
Aim is to compare envelope protein amino-acid substitutions and selection pressures between WNV lineage 2 populations in historical and novel geographical ranges.

The main objectives of this study were to:

- Characterise envelope amino-acid diversity in both geographical ranges.
- Identify envelope codon sites showing evidence of positive selection.
- Map selected substitutions onto a three-dimensional envelope protein structure.
- Assess whether variable residues occur in functionally important regions.

## Results

The analysis identified differences in envelope protein amino-acid variation between historical and novel WNV lineage 2 populations. Substitutions at codon 159 were highly prevalent in the novel range but absent from the historical range. MEME identified sites with evidence of episodic diversifying selection, while FEL and FUBAR did not identify evidence of pervasive positive selection. V188I and V488I were recurrent substitutions observed in historical-range sequences.

## Pipeline used in this project

The study involved the retrieval and processing of WNV lineage 2 genomic sequences, followed by envelope protein mutation analysis, selection analysis and structural investigation.

The workflow included:

- Sequence retrieval and geographical classification.
- Lineage confirmation and sequence quality assessment.
- Envelope protein amino-acid substitution analysis.
- Codon-based selection analysis.
- Three-dimensional protein structural modelling and visualisation.


## Tools and Software

The following tools and software were used in this project:

- **NCBI Virus:** Retrieval of WNV genomic sequences and associated metadata.
- **Genome Detective:** Confirmation of WNV lineage.
- **Nextclade:** Sequence quality control, clade assignment and mutation calling.
- **MAFFT:** Multiple sequence alignment.
- **AliView:** Visualisation and inspection of sequence alignments.
- **Python:** Sequence processing and data analysis.
- **RStudio:** Creation of figures and data visualisation.
- **HyPhy:** Codon-based selection analysis using MEME, FEL and FUBAR, and recombination analysis using GARD.
- **ColabFold/AlphaFold2:** Three-dimensional envelope protein structure prediction.
- **RCSB Mol* 3D Viewer:** Visualisation of protein structures and mapping of amino-acid substitutions.
